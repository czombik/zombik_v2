Chris is a writer and independent educational consultant. He previously lived and worked in Shanghai, China, where he attended the Shanghai Writing Workshop. He has written numerous stories, including three sci-fi novellas for the International 3-Day Novel Contest, and co-authored the memoir of a business associate from China. He is curently editing his first novel, which is epic fantasy. In his free time, Chris runs a homebrew D&D campaign, runs [Somerville Shut Up & Write](https://www.meetup.com/shutupwritebostonarea/events/312760840/?utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=share-btn_savedevents_share_modal&utm_source=link&utm_version=v2&member_id=188668930), does yoga, drinks coffee, and tries to learn as much as he can about everything. He lives in Somerville, Massachusetts.
